Data points that can be reported on are easy to find. How many people clicked on the phishing test? How many emails were blocked by the spam filter? The challenge arises when you want to move beyond security theatre and towards data that actually tells you something meaningful about your security posture. 

This presentation will highlight some examples of poor metrics and, hopefully, some more useful ones – providing some food for thought!
