AI has completely changed offensive security and application security, but a huge amount of defensive cybersecurity work looks basically the same as it did a year ago. That is going to change.

This talk explains why AI is soon going to take over a huge amount of detection work. It will also cover the parts AI is extremely good at already, the parts that suck today, some of the tricks for using AI for detection, and what developments we still need in order for AI to truly take over.
